## General defense-craft (applies to any question)

- **Answer the question actually asked** — don't launch a mini-lecture. Short, direct, then stop.
- **If you don't know, say so gracefully:** "I didn't test that directly, but my expectation is X
  because Y — it would be a good extension." Never bluff a fabricated number.
- **Buy thinking time** by restating the question: "So you're asking whether the pixel-spacing
  variation could create a spurious signal — no, and here's why…"
- **Bridge back to strengths.** Almost every methods question can end at your master framing: the
  positive control and "every bias is upward, still null → conservative."
- **Concede real limitations confidently.** You disclosed most of them yourself. Owning a limitation
  reads as competence; getting defensive reads as insecurity.
- **Watch your pace and volume.** Nerves speed you up. Deliberately slow down, especially on the
  key numbers.
